Notes to mariners

Passage
Four openings: three tidal gates and the powered lock against the east wall. With the gates shut, everything locks through on the east. With them open the lock stands open too, so the only passage is southbound, through the middle tidal gate between the flared guidewalls. There is no protected way north until the gates shut.
Timing
Gate state is predicted from published NOAA tide data using a fixed offset to local high water. A closed window of six hours alternates with roughly six and a half hours open.
On scene
Lock operator monitors VHF 13 · 631‑852‑8299. Three horn blasts to request the lock; green light to proceed.

This is a prediction, not an observation. Wind and storm surge shift real tides by an hour or more, and mechanical failure or county maintenance overrides the schedule entirely — the canal has closed to all traffic on short notice before.

Not for navigation. Confirm on scene or by radio before committing to the cut. Predictions have not been verified against logged gate operations; treat the timing as approximate and widen your margin near a transition.
